# Code of Conduct

This project expects respectful, technically constructive participation.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person presenting them.
- Give specific, actionable feedback.
- Respect different levels of experience and background.
- Keep public discussions relevant to the project.
- Do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, repeated personal attacks, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and disclosure of private information are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.

For a private conduct report, use the repository's private vulnerability reporting form and begin the report title with `Conduct`. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue. Reports will be handled as privately as the circumstances allow.
